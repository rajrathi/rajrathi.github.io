I build machine learning systems and the data infrastructure that keeps them fed.

I graduated from the **College of Engineering, Pune** in 2020 with a B.Tech in
Computer Science. Two internships shaped what I wanted to work on: the first, at
VInventTech, was a computer-vision system that flagged two-wheeler riders not
wearing helmets; the second, at **Veritas**, was an impact-analysis engine that
mapped the dependencies between microservices and scored what would break if any
one of them went down.

Since graduating I have been at **Bitwise**, working across several clients.
The first couple of years were data engineering — pipelines, warehouses and the
unglamorous plumbing that makes analytics trustworthy. I now work as a machine
learning engineer for a payment technology client. Along the way I finished a PG
Diploma in Data Science at **IIIT Bangalore**, which is where a long-standing
interest in deep learning turned into something I could actually ship.

Day to day that means Python, PySpark and Airflow; Azure and AWS; PyTorch and
TensorFlow when there is a model to train. I care about systems that are boring
to operate and easy to reason about six months later.

This site is also where I write. Expect posts on applied AI, data science and
cloud engineering — the things I had to figure out the hard way, written down so
the next person does not have to.
