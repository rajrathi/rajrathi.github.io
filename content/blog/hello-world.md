I have rebuilt this site. The old one worked, but it had drifted into being a
static résumé that happened to have a blog attached. I wanted the opposite: a
place I write in regularly, with the résumé alongside it.

## What I am going to write about

Three threads, mostly:

**Applied machine learning.** Not paper reviews — I am not a researcher and there
are people far better at that. What interests me is the distance between a model
that works in a notebook and one that works on a Tuesday at 3am. Feature stores,
training pipelines, drift, the retraining loop, the parts nobody demos.

**Data engineering.** I spent my first years out of university building ETL, and
I still think it is the most underrated skill in the field. A model is only as
honest as the table underneath it. Expect posts about Spark, Airflow, warehouse
modelling, and making batch jobs boring enough to ignore.

**Cloud.** Azure and AWS, mostly from the perspective of someone who has to pay
attention to both cost and the on-call rota.

## How I want to write it

Short, specific, and with the working shown. The posts I get the most from are
the ones where somebody hit a wall, explained the wall, and explained how they
got over it. That is the bar I am aiming at. If a post could have been a
paragraph, it will be a paragraph.

Everything here is plain Markdown in a Git repository, which is deliberate — the
friction of publishing should be roughly zero, or I will not do it.
